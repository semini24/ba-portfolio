01 / THE CHALLENGE

Great places.
Scattered information.

✳

Visitors exploring Sri Lanka's heritage often need to switch between sources for destination information, route planning, facilities, and safety assistance.

The team set out to bring heritage discovery, tour planning, route previews, historical guidance, and location-based support into one mobile experience. The solution was designed for tourists, local visitors, and people interested in cultural sites.

✦
Research signal

18 of 30 initial survey respondents (60%) rated personalized itinerary planning 4 or 5 out of 5. Participants also highlighted route efficiency, historical information, facilities, and safety needs.

Source: Milestone 02 report, research recap.

Stakeholders considered

Tourists & visitorsLocal usersHeritage-site visitorsTourism service providersSystem administrators

06 / WHAT I LEARNED

Good analysis begins
with real people.

HeritageWalk gave me practical experience in requirements analysis, Figma/wireframe design, and functional/usability testing. I learned to connect user research to explicit requirements, translate those needs into understandable user flows, and analyze evidence to guide improvements.

It also reinforced the value of distinguishing working functionality from an intuitive user experience—a lesson I want to carry into future Business Analyst projects.
